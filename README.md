## Why bioinformatics?

Because I love biology. Because I love medicine. And because biology
is drowning in data that needs someone willing to *look* at it.

But there's another reason. I like **order**.
I like taking a mess and slowly turning it into something that makes sense.
I like digging through the noise until a pattern emerges. I like asking
a question and getting an answer, even if the answer is "no".

Bioinformatics, to me, feels like that: **analysis in service of answers**.
Not just coding for the sake of coding, but coding to understand something
about life. That's what draws me in.

I'm not sure yet if it's my future. That's the whole point of trying.
I'd rather spend a year figuring out that I don't like something,
than spend a lifetime wondering "what if".
